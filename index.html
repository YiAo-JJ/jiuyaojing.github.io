About Me

I received my B. Eng from Changchun University of Science and Technology and am currently a Ph.D. student at the School of Cyber Engineering, Xidian University. My research focuses on face security, with an emphasis on developing generalized face forgery detection or anti-spoofing models. My current research goals are focused on vision-language models, generalized detection, and test-time adaptation.
